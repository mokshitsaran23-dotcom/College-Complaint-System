# Task: SCRUM02-F002-UI-002 — Maintenance staff assigned-complaints view

## Objective
Ensure maintenance technicians view only work orders assigned to their designated department.

## User Story
> **As a maintenance staff member, I want to see complaints assigned to my department, so that I know what work needs to be done.**

## Acceptance Criteria
- **AC1**: Given a staff member logs in, when they open their queue, then only complaints assigned to their department are shown.

## Dependencies
- Role-based access control (RBAC) & Authentication service
